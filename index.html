How do you play Would You Rather?

Pick a category, read the card out loud, and everyone answers. Two impossible choices, one room, nobody gets to stay quiet. It works with two players or ten, and it works with no internet.

How it works

Open the app and pick a category. A card appears with two options. Read it to the room, tap your answer, and see how the group split. Then the next card. There is no timer and no score — the game is the conversation the card starts.

560 questions across 7 categories, with new questions added regularly.

Common questions

Do I need internet to play?

No. The game works fully offline. There are no accounts and no sign ups.

How many people can play?

Two to ten works best, but there is no hard limit. One phone is passed around or held by whoever is reading.

What is free and what is Pro?

Would You Rather, Party Classic and Friends are free. Pro unlocks all 7 categories, removes the cap on cards per round, and removes ads.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Would You Rather and cancel there. Cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep access until the current period ends.

I paid and the app still shows a lock.

Tap Restore on the Pro screen first — that re-checks your Apple account. If it still shows locked, write to us with the Apple ID email you purchased under and we will sort it.
